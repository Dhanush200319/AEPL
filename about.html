About Company / Overview

Aerospace Engineers Pvt. Ltd. (The Salem Aeropark) is a multi-disciplinary engineering and manufacturing organization offering products and services across aircraft components, defense & missile systems, space systems, healthcare components, and renewable energy.

With over 25 years of experience, we position ourselves as a full-spectrum engineering and manufacturing group serving aviation, space, missile, and healthcare industries. Our state-of-the-art facilities and highly skilled workforce enable us to deliver cutting-edge solutions that meet the most challenging requirements of our global clientele.

1995

Company Founded

Aerospace Engineers Pvt. Ltd. was established in Salem, Tamil Nadu, with a vision to serve the growing aerospace industry in India. Starting with a small workshop and a team of passionate engineers, our journey began with a commitment to precision and excellence that would define our future.

Company Foundation

2000

First Major Contract

Secured our first major contract with a global aerospace manufacturer, establishing our position in the industry for precision manufacturing and quality excellence. This contract marked our entry into the international market and validated our capabilities as a reliable partner.

5 Employees
1 Major Contract
5 CNC Machines
Global Contract

2005

Expansion & Growth

Expanded operations with new facilities and advanced machinery. Began manufacturing components for defense and missile systems while maintaining our focus on aerospace excellence. This expansion allowed us to serve a broader range of clients and products in the defense sector.

Growth Era

2010

ISO 9001 Certified

Achieved ISO 9001 certification, demonstrating our commitment to quality management. This milestone positioned us for international partnerships and global contracts. This certification validated our processes and opened doors to larger international projects.

Quality Certified

2015

AS9100 Certified

Obtained AS9100 certification, the industry standard for quality management in aviation, space, and defense. This achievement validated our aerospace-specific quality processes. This certification reinforced our position as a trusted supplier in the aerospace industry.

Aerospace Excellence

2018

Space Systems Division

Established our Space Systems Division to serve the growing global space industry, developing components for satellite and launch vehicle applications. This expansion into space technology positioned us at the forefront of aerospace innovation.

Space Division

2020

Healthcare Innovation

Expanded into healthcare components during the pandemic, leveraging our precision manufacturing capabilities to support essential medical device production. This pivot demonstrated our adaptability and commitment to societal needs beyond aerospace.

Healthcare Support

2023

Global Recognition

Reached 25+ countries served and received the Excellence in Manufacturing Award from the Indian Aerospace Association, marking our position as a global leader. Our components now support aerospace projects across multiple continents.

Global Leader

2025

Future Vision

Continuing to innovate in sustainable aerospace technologies, advanced materials, and smart manufacturing. Committed to supporting next-generation aerospace initiatives globally. Our focus remains on sustainable practices and cutting-edge technology integration.
