AEROSPACE TIMELINE

A Journey Through Innovation and Excellence

1998
FOUNDATION
Aerospace Engineers Pvt. Ltd. founded in Salem, focusing on rubber compounds and engineered components for aviation industry.
2003
EXPANSION
First major contract with defense sector, expanding product range to include missile components and military-grade materials.
2008
INTERNATIONAL GROWTH
Establishment of international partnerships, exporting products to 10+ countries across Asia, Europe, and North America.
2012
TECHNOLOGY ADVANCEMENT
Investment in advanced manufacturing technologies including automation, robotics, and AI-powered quality control systems.
2016
SPACE SYSTEMS DIVISION
Launch of space systems division, entering satellite and space exploration markets with specialized components.
2020
HEALTHCARE INNOVATION
Expansion into healthcare sector with medical-grade rubber components and COVID-19 protective equipment manufacturing.
2023
BOEING PARTNERSHIP
Significant milestone: MoU with Boeing for manufacturing certain aerospace components, validating our global capabilities.
